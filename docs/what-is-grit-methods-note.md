# What Is Grit? — Methods Note

This folder supports reproducible examples for the article **What Is Grit?**

The workflows use synthetic data to illustrate how grit can be modeled as a combination of:

- perseverance of effort
- consistency of interest
- conscientiousness
- social support
- prior achievement
- achievement-related outcomes

The examples are designed for learning, article support, and reproducible research demonstration. They are not intended for evaluating, ranking, selecting, hiring, disciplining, or psychologically assessing real people.

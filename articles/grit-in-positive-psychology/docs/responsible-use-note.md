# Responsible Use Note

These files use synthetic data and are intended for article support, reproducible research education, and methodological demonstration.

They should not be used for:

- hiring
- admissions
- discipline
- employee evaluation
- student ranking
- psychological diagnosis
- individual assessment
- workplace surveillance
- predicting real individual achievement
- claiming that grit alone explains flourishing

Grit in positive psychology should be interpreted with attention to measurement limits, construct overlap, social support, institutional context, health, depletion, relationships, meaning, and the risk of turning persistence into a moral burden.

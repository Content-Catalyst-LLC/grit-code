# Methods Note

This article folder demonstrates why perseverance of effort and consistency of interests should often be interpreted separately.

A simple total grit model can be written as:

\[
G_i = w_P P_i + w_C C_i
\]

where \(G_i\) is total grit, \(P_i\) is perseverance of effort, and \(C_i\) is consistency of interests.

A facet-specific outcome model can be written as:

\[
Y_i = \beta_0 + \beta_1 P_i + \beta_2 C_i + \beta_3 A_i + \beta_4 S_i + \epsilon_i
\]

where \(Y_i\) is an outcome, \(A_i\) is prior achievement or ability, and \(S_i\) is support or context.

The article’s core measurement point is that a total grit score may hide different developmental profiles:

- high effort / high consistency
- high effort / low consistency
- low effort / high consistency
- low effort / low consistency

The workflows use synthetic data and should be interpreted as methodological demonstrations only.

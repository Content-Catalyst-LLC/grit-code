# Responsible Use Note

These files use synthetic data and are intended for article support, reproducible research education, and methodological demonstration.

They should not be used for:

- hiring
- admissions
- discipline
- employee evaluation
- student ranking
- psychological diagnosis
- individual assessment
- workplace surveillance
- predicting real individual achievement

Perseverance of effort and consistency of interests should be interpreted with attention to measurement limits, construct overlap, developmental stage, exploration, burnout, social support, prior achievement, institutional context, and the risk of turning persistence into a moral burden.

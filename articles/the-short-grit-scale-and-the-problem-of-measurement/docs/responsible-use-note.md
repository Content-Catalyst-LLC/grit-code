# Responsible Use Note

These files use synthetic data and are intended for article support, reproducible research education, and methodological demonstration.

They should not be used for:

- hiring
- admissions
- discipline
- employee evaluation
- student ranking
- psychological diagnosis
- individual assessment
- workplace surveillance
- predicting real individual achievement
- reproducing copyrighted scale items

The Short Grit Scale should be interpreted with attention to measurement limits, short-form tradeoffs, construct overlap, social support, prior achievement, institutional context, and the risk of turning persistence into a moral burden.

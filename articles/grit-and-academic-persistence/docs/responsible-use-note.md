# Responsible Use Note

These files use synthetic data and are intended for article support, reproducible research education, and methodological demonstration.

They should not be used for:

- admissions
- hiring
- student ranking
- discipline
- employee evaluation
- psychological diagnosis
- individual assessment
- workplace surveillance
- predicting real student outcomes

Grit and academic persistence should be interpreted with attention to measurement limits, instructional quality, belonging, social support, financial stress, health, disability access, prior preparation, feedback, institutional trust, discrimination, burnout, and the risk of turning persistence into a moral burden.

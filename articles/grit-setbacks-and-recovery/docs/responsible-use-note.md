# Responsible Use Note

These files use synthetic data and are intended for article support, reproducible research education, and methodological demonstration.

They should not be used for:

- admissions
- hiring
- student ranking
- discipline
- employee evaluation
- psychological diagnosis
- individual assessment
- workplace surveillance
- predicting real individual outcomes

Grit, setbacks, and recovery should be interpreted with attention to context, support, recovery resources, trauma, institutional responsibility, health, disability access, financial stress, burnout, and the risk of romanticizing harm as character-building.

# Ethics and Responsible Use Note

## Article

**Designing Environments That Support Grit**

## Appropriate use

These materials are appropriate for:

- synthetic-data demonstration
- research-method teaching
- positive psychology article support
- environmental-design evaluation
- program-evaluation examples
- psychometrics education
- responsible interpretation training

## Inappropriate use

Do not use these materials for:

- hiring
- admissions
- student ranking
- employee evaluation
- psychological diagnosis
- individual assessment
- discipline
- workplace surveillance
- predicting individual achievement
- deciding who deserves support
- excusing low expectations
- excusing harmful institutions
- pressuring people to endure harmful conditions

## Ethical principles

Professional use should protect:

- autonomy
- consent
- privacy
- dignity
- cultural context
- developmental appropriateness
- disability access
- fairness
- wellbeing
- recovery
- interpretive humility
- institutional accountability

## Responsible interpretation

Grit-supportive design should not use positive psychology language to hide overload, unfairness, exclusion, or poor institutional design. The environment should be evaluated alongside the person.

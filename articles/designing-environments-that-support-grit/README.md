# Designing Environments That Support Grit

This article folder supports reproducible research examples for **Designing Environments That Support Grit** in the Grit knowledge series.

The scaffold is designed for professional positive psychologists, educational researchers, organizational psychologists, program evaluators, psychometricians, coaches working in evidence-informed settings, and applied researchers studying how environments shape adaptive persistence.

## Research focus

The article examines how environments can be intentionally designed to support grit without coercion, burnout, overpersistence, or institutional blame-shifting.

This folder supports research and evaluation around:

- environmental design for adaptive persistence
- autonomy support
- competence support
- feedback quality
- belonging
- mentoring access
- psychological safety
- recovery design
- material resources
- fairness and transparency
- credible opportunity pathways
- adaptive quitting norms
- chronic stress
- blocked opportunity
- burnout risk
- goal progress
- wellbeing
- person-environment interaction
- professional ethics and responsible interpretation

## Professional framing

A grit-supportive environment does not merely demand perseverance. It designs the conditions that make sustained effort meaningful, informed, recoverable, dignified, and connected to real progress.

## Repository purpose

This scaffold is designed for synthetic-data demonstration, research education, article support, reproducible workflow design, professional interpretation training, and positive psychology program-evaluation examples.

It is not a psychological test, screening tool, diagnostic system, hiring tool, admissions tool, student-ranking system, employee-evaluation system, or individual assessment instrument.

## Folder structure

- `docs/` — construct definition, environmental-design framework, measurement notes, ethics, limitations, and responsible-use notes
- `data/raw/` — synthetic environmental-design survey data and data dictionary
- `data/processed/` — generated modeled datasets
- `python/` — environmental design modeling, psychometrics, moderation, and burnout-safety workflows
- `r/` — parallel positive psychology workflows
- `sql/` — research schema and analytical views
- `julia/` — compact environmental-design simulation
- `c/`, `cpp/`, `fortran/`, `go`, `rust` — small computational examples
- `notebooks/` — professional notebook plan
- `outputs/tables/` — generated tables
- `outputs/figures/` — generated figures

## Professional caution

Environmental-design research should not be used to excuse low expectations or erase individual agency. It should also not be used to blame individuals for outcomes shaped by poor instruction, unsafe institutions, discrimination, inaccessible systems, unsupported caregiving, poverty, workload overload, or lack of opportunity.

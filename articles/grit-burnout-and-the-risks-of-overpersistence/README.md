# Grit, Burnout, and the Risks of Overpersistence

This article folder supports reproducible research examples for **Grit, Burnout, and the Risks of Overpersistence** in the Grit knowledge series.

The workflows use synthetic data to demonstrate how persistence can become harmful when effort loses contact with recovery, feedback, autonomy, goal fit, and health:

- perseverance of effort
- consistency of interests
- grit
- demand intensity
- goal rigidity
- identity pressure
- sunk cost
- recovery capacity
- social support
- autonomy
- feedback responsiveness
- goal fit
- overpersistence
- burnout risk
- sustainable persistence
- responsible interpretation

## Repository purpose

This scaffold is designed for article support, reproducible learning, research-method demonstration, and transparent computational examples. It is not designed for ranking, admissions, hiring, discipline, psychological diagnosis, student evaluation, employee evaluation, or assessment of real people.

## Folder structure

- `python/` — Python workflow for modeling grit, burnout, and overpersistence
- `r/` — R workflow for overpersistence profiles and burnout risk
- `julia/` — Julia synthetic-data sketch
- `sql/` — SQL schema and analytical views
- `c/`, `cpp/`, `fortran/`, `go/`, `rust/` — compact computational examples
- `data/raw/` — small synthetic example dataset
- `data/processed/` — generated modeled datasets
- `docs/` — methods note, responsible-use note, and WordPress GitHub embed block
- `notebooks/` — notebook planning notes
- `outputs/figures/` — generated figures
- `outputs/tables/` — generated tables

## Responsible use

These workflows use synthetic data. They should not be used to assess, score, select, admit, discipline, rank, hire, promote, or psychologically evaluate real people.

The examples are intended to clarify that grit is not unlimited effort. Sustainable persistence depends on recovery, feedback, autonomy, goal fit, social support, institutional conditions, and the wisdom to revise or exit when continued effort becomes harmful.

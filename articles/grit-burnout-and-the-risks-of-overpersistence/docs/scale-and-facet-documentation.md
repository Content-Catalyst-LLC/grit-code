# Scale and Facet Documentation

## Article

**Grit, Burnout, and the Risks of Overpersistence**

## Synthetic scale family

The synthetic dataset in this folder includes illustrative items for:

- perseverance of effort
- consistency of interests
- purpose alignment
- recovery capacity
- feedback responsiveness
- social support
- burnout risk
- adaptive persistence
- long-term goal commitment

## Example facet interpretation

### Perseverance of effort

Represents sustained behavioral effort when progress is slow, difficult, or uncertain.

Professional caution: high effort may be adaptive or harmful depending on recovery, autonomy, goal fit, and institutional context.

### Consistency of interests

Represents relative stability of interests and goal commitments over time.

Professional caution: lower consistency can reflect healthy exploration, especially in adolescence and emerging adulthood.

### Recovery capacity

Represents emotional, cognitive, physical, and social resources that support return after setbacks.

Professional caution: recovery is partly contextual and should not be treated as a purely individual trait.

### Feedback responsiveness

Represents willingness and capacity to use information to revise strategy.

Professional caution: feedback must be actionable, fair, and psychologically safe.

### Adaptive persistence

Represents sustained effort that remains responsive to feedback, recovery, health, purpose, and ethics.

Professional caution: adaptive persistence is distinct from overpersistence, compliance, or endurance under harmful conditions.

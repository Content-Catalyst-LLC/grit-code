# Responsible Use Note

These files use synthetic data and are intended for article support, reproducible research education, and methodological demonstration.

They should not be used for:

- admissions
- hiring
- student ranking
- discipline
- employee evaluation
- psychological diagnosis
- individual assessment
- workplace surveillance
- predicting real individual outcomes

Grit, burnout, and overpersistence should be interpreted with attention to context, workload, recovery, autonomy, social support, feedback, goal fit, health, disability access, financial stress, organizational responsibility, and the risk of using grit language to pressure people into harmful endurance.

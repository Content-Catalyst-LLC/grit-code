# Grit and Narrative Identity

This article folder supports reproducible research examples for **Grit and Narrative Identity** in the Grit knowledge series.

The workflows use synthetic data to demonstrate how grit can be modeled alongside narrative identity as a meaning-making system that supports long-term persistence:

- perseverance of effort
- consistency of interests
- grit
- narrative coherence
- agency
- meaning-making
- future orientation
- narrative identity
- social support
- feedback quality
- opportunity access
- health stability
- institutional trust
- burnout
- narrative strain
- long-term persistence
- responsible interpretation

## Repository purpose

This scaffold is designed for article support, reproducible learning, research-method demonstration, and transparent computational examples. It is not designed for ranking, admissions, hiring, discipline, psychological diagnosis, student evaluation, employee evaluation, or assessment of real people.

## Folder structure

- `python/` — Python workflow for modeling grit, narrative coherence, and persistence
- `r/` — R workflow for narrative profiles and long-term commitment
- `julia/` — Julia synthetic-data sketch
- `sql/` — SQL schema and analytical views
- `c/`, `cpp/`, `fortran/`, `go/`, `rust/` — compact computational examples
- `data/raw/` — small synthetic example dataset
- `data/processed/` — generated modeled datasets
- `docs/` — methods note, responsible-use note, and WordPress GitHub embed block
- `notebooks/` — notebook planning notes
- `outputs/figures/` — generated figures
- `outputs/tables/` — generated tables

## Responsible use

These workflows use synthetic data. They should not be used to assess, score, select, admit, discipline, rank, hire, promote, or psychologically evaluate real people.

The examples are intended to clarify how narrative identity may help give grit continuity, meaning, agency, and future orientation while long-term persistence still depends on support, feedback, opportunity, institutional trust, health, recovery, and social conditions.

# Responsible Use Note

These files use synthetic data and are intended for article support, reproducible research education, and methodological demonstration.

They should not be used for:

- admissions
- hiring
- student ranking
- discipline
- employee evaluation
- psychological diagnosis
- individual assessment
- workplace surveillance
- predicting real individual outcomes

Grit and narrative identity should be interpreted with attention to consent, privacy, trauma, cultural context, institutional trust, social support, opportunity access, health, burnout, narrative strain, and the risk of forcing people to turn hardship into inspirational or productivity-oriented stories.

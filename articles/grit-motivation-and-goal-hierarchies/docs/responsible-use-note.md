# Responsible Use Note

These files use synthetic data and are intended for article support, reproducible research education, and methodological demonstration.

They should not be used for:

- hiring
- admissions
- discipline
- employee evaluation
- student ranking
- psychological diagnosis
- individual assessment
- workplace surveillance
- predicting real individual achievement

Grit, motivation, and goal hierarchy should be interpreted with attention to measurement limits, goal conflict, support, feedback, burnout, institutional context, unequal opportunity, and the risk of turning persistence or purpose into a moral burden.

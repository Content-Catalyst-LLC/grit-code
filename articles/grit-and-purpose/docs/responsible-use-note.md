# Responsible Use Note

These files use synthetic data and are intended for article support, reproducible research education, and methodological demonstration.

They should not be used for:

- admissions
- hiring
- student ranking
- discipline
- employee evaluation
- psychological diagnosis
- individual assessment
- workplace surveillance
- predicting real individual outcomes

Grit and purpose should be interpreted with attention to measurement limits, autonomy, coercion, institutional context, social support, opportunity access, health, burnout, moral overcommitment, and the risk of turning meaningful work into self-sacrifice or blame.

# Can Grit Be Taught?

This article folder supports reproducible research examples for **Can Grit Be Taught?** in the Grit knowledge series.

The scaffold is designed for professional positive psychologists, psychometricians, educational researchers, program evaluators, coaches working in evidence-informed settings, and applied researchers who need transparent materials for construct definition, measurement, intervention design, and responsible interpretation.

## Research focus

The article asks whether grit can be taught and answers in a qualified professional sense: adaptive persistence can be developed when people have meaningful goals, credible pathways, feedback, practice, support, recovery, autonomy, and environments where effort can actually lead somewhere.

This folder supports research and evaluation around:

- grit intervention design
- adaptive persistence
- perseverance of effort
- consistency of interests
- goal clarity
- purpose alignment
- feedback responsiveness
- recovery capacity
- self-regulation planning
- social support
- burnout-risk monitoring
- overpersistence safeguards
- adaptive quitting
- intervention moderation by context
- professional ethics and responsible use

## Repository purpose

This scaffold is designed for synthetic-data demonstration, research education, article support, reproducible workflow design, professional interpretation training, and positive psychology program-evaluation examples.

It is not a psychological test, screening tool, diagnostic system, hiring tool, admissions tool, student-ranking system, employee-evaluation system, or individual assessment instrument.

## Folder structure

- `docs/` — construct definition, intervention design, ethics, psychometrics, limitations, and responsible-use notes
- `data/raw/` — synthetic survey-style intervention dataset and codebook
- `data/processed/` — generated modeled datasets
- `python/` — psychometrics, intervention evaluation, and moderation workflows
- `r/` — parallel positive psychology evaluation workflows
- `sql/` — research schema and analytical views
- `julia/` — compact intervention simulation
- `c/`, `cpp/`, `fortran/`, `go`, `rust` — small computational examples
- `notebooks/` — professional notebook plan
- `outputs/tables/` — generated tables
- `outputs/figures/` — generated figures

## Professional caution

A grit intervention should not be used to blame people for outcomes shaped by poverty, discrimination, trauma, poor instruction, unsafe institutions, inaccessible systems, unsupported caregiving, or lack of opportunity. Any real-world intervention should include validated measures, implementation documentation, ethics review where applicable, participant protections, and harm monitoring.

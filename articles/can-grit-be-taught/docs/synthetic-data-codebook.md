- `age` — synthetic age
- `developmental_stage` — adolescence, emerging_adulthood, adulthood, later_adulthood
- `condition` — comparison or grit_support
- `implementation_quality` — synthetic quality of intervention delivery
- `baseline_perseverance` — synthetic baseline perseverance score
- `baseline_consistency` — synthetic baseline consistency score
- `baseline_adaptive_persistence` — synthetic baseline adaptive persistence
- `baseline_support` — synthetic baseline support
- `baseline_stress` — synthetic baseline stress
- `baseline_recovery` — synthetic baseline recovery capacity
- `baseline_burnout_risk` — synthetic baseline burnout-risk score
- `post_perseverance` — synthetic post-intervention perseverance
- `post_consistency` — synthetic post-intervention consistency
- `post_adaptive_persistence` — synthetic post-intervention adaptive persistence
- `post_purpose_alignment` — synthetic post-intervention purpose alignment
- `post_feedback_responsiveness` — synthetic post-intervention feedback responsiveness
- `post_recovery_capacity` — synthetic post-intervention recovery capacity
- `post_burnout_risk` — synthetic post-intervention burnout risk
- `post_wellbeing` — synthetic post-intervention wellbeing
- `goal_progress` — synthetic goal progress
- `intervention_acceptability` — synthetic acceptability score

## Scale caution

These variables are not validated psychological measures. They are synthetic fields for workflow demonstration.

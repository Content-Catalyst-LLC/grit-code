# Responsible Use Note

These files use synthetic data and are intended for article support, reproducible research education, and methodological demonstration.

They should not be used for:

- hiring
- admissions
- discipline
- employee evaluation
- student ranking
- psychological diagnosis
- individual assessment
- workplace surveillance
- predicting real individual achievement

Grit and deliberate practice should be interpreted with attention to measurement limits, practice access, coaching quality, prior skill, support, recovery, burnout, institutional context, and the risk of turning effort or practice into a moral burden.

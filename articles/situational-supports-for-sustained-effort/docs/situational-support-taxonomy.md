# Situational Support Taxonomy

## Article

**Situational Supports for Sustained Effort**

## Support domains

### 1. Autonomy support

Indicators:

- meaningful choice
- clear rationale
- respect for perspective
- noncoercive goal framing
- ownership of effort
- voice in strategy and revision

### 2. Competence support

Indicators:

- actionable feedback
- clear standards
- visible progress
- scaffolded challenge
- practice structure
- revision opportunities

### 3. Relatedness and belonging

Indicators:

- mentoring access
- peer support
- recognition
- inclusion
- social trust
- legitimacy in the setting

### 4. Psychological safety

Indicators:

- ability to ask questions
- ability to admit mistakes
- ability to seek help
- respectful correction
- protection from humiliation
- learning-oriented climate

### 5. Recovery support

Indicators:

- rest
- pacing
- workload realism
- emotional support
- health access
- boundary norms

### 6. Material and structural support

Indicators:

- time
- tools
- transportation
- financial stability
- childcare or caregiving support
- accessibility and accommodations
- stable routines

### 7. Fairness and opportunity

Indicators:

- transparent evaluation
- real advancement pathways
- credible link between effort and progress
- bias monitoring
- accessible support
- institutional accountability

## Professional caution

Situational supports should be measured alongside individual grit to avoid blaming people for outcomes shaped by structural barriers or poorly designed environments.

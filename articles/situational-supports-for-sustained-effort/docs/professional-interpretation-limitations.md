# Professional Interpretation and Limitations

## Article

**Situational Supports for Sustained Effort**

## What this scaffold can support

The synthetic workflows can illustrate:

- how to represent situational supports in research data
- how to model adaptive persistence as a person-environment process
- how to test whether support moderates the relationship between grit and effort
- how to model burnout risk as a safety outcome
- how to estimate associations among support, goal progress, and wellbeing
- how to document limits and ethical cautions

## What this scaffold cannot support

These materials cannot determine:

- whether a real person has enough grit
- whether a real person deserves support
- whether an institution is fair without real-world audit evidence
- whether a program is effective without proper evaluation
- whether someone should be admitted, hired, promoted, disciplined, or evaluated
- whether persistence is adaptive without contextual evidence

## Professional limitation

Situational supports are complex. They include subjective perceptions, material realities, institutional rules, cultural meanings, and relational conditions. Quantitative scores should be interpreted with qualitative context and professional judgment.

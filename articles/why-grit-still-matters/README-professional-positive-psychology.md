# Professional Positive Psychology Research Scaffold

## Article

**Why Grit Still Matters**

## Purpose

This scaffold turns the article folder into a professional positive-psychology research repository focused on grit as adaptive persistence. It preserves the value of grit while integrating the strongest criticisms: measurement limits, overlap with conscientiousness and self-control, meta-analytic caution, burnout risk, adaptive quitting, context, equity, and institutional responsibility.

## Professional assets included

- adaptive persistence synthesis
- construct definitions
- measurement and psychometrics notes
- synthetic survey-style data
- data dictionary
- Python and R workflows for adaptive-persistence modeling, moderation, goal progress, burnout safety, wellbeing, and construct comparison
- SQL schema for organizing capstone grit research data
- responsible-use and ethics notes
- professional interpretation limitations
- notebook plan for research reporting

## Appropriate use

Use this repository for:

- synthetic-data demonstration
- research-method teaching
- professional positive psychology article support
- adaptive-persistence modeling
- psychometrics education
- intervention and program-evaluation examples
- responsible-use documentation

## Inappropriate use

Do not use this repository for:

- psychological diagnosis
- individual assessment
- employee evaluation
- student ranking
- hiring
- admissions
- discipline
- workplace surveillance
- predicting real individual outcomes
- deciding who deserves support
- justifying harmful persistence
- excusing harmful institutions

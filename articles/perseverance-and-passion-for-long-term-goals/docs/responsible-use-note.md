# Responsible Use Note

These files use synthetic data and are intended for article support, reproducible research education, and methodological demonstration.

They should not be used for:

- hiring
- admissions
- discipline
- employee evaluation
- student ranking
- psychological diagnosis
- individual assessment
- workplace surveillance
- predicting real individual achievement

Grit research should be interpreted with attention to measurement limits, construct overlap, opportunity, support, institutional context, and the risks of turning persistence into a moral burden.

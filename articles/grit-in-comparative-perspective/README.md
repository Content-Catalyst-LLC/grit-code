# Grit in Comparative Perspective

This article folder supports reproducible research examples for **Grit in Comparative Perspective** in the Grit knowledge series.

The scaffold is designed for professional positive psychologists, personality researchers, psychometricians, educational researchers, organizational psychologists, program evaluators, and applied researchers who need transparent materials for comparing grit with neighboring constructs.

## Research focus

The article places grit alongside related constructs rather than treating it as a standalone master predictor. It supports professional comparison among:

- grit
- perseverance of effort
- consistency of interests
- self-control
- conscientiousness
- resilience and recovery
- deliberate practice quality
- motivation
- purpose alignment
- growth mindset
- narrative identity
- environmental support
- adaptive persistence
- goal progress
- burnout risk
- wellbeing

## Professional framing

Grit matters, but it should be interpreted modestly and comparatively. It overlaps with self-control, conscientiousness, motivation, purpose, deliberate practice, resilience, and environmental support. A professional account asks what grit uniquely contributes, what neighboring constructs explain, and when context changes the interpretation.

## Repository purpose

This scaffold is designed for synthetic-data demonstration, research education, article support, reproducible workflow design, professional interpretation training, and positive psychology research-method examples.

It is not a psychological test, screening tool, diagnostic system, hiring tool, admissions tool, student-ranking system, employee-evaluation system, or individual assessment instrument.

## Folder structure

- `docs/` — construct comparison, measurement notes, ethics, limitations, and responsible-use notes
- `data/raw/` — synthetic comparative-construct survey data and data dictionary
- `data/processed/` — generated modeled datasets
- `python/` — comparative construct modeling, psychometrics, overlap, moderation, and burnout-safety workflows
- `r/` — parallel positive psychology workflows
- `sql/` — research schema and analytical views
- `julia/` — compact comparative-construct simulation
- `c/`, `cpp/`, `fortran/`, `go`, `rust` — small computational examples
- `notebooks/` — professional notebook plan
- `outputs/tables/` — generated tables
- `outputs/figures/` — generated figures

## Professional caution

Comparative construct analysis should not be used to rank individuals, make employment or admissions decisions, or assign moral worth. It is for understanding construct overlap, developmental interpretation, responsible measurement, and context-sensitive research.

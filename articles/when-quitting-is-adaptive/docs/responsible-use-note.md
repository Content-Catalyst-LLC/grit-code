# Responsible Use Note

These files use synthetic data and are intended for article support, reproducible research education, and methodological demonstration.

They should not be used for:

- admissions
- hiring
- student ranking
- discipline
- employee evaluation
- psychological diagnosis
- individual assessment
- workplace surveillance
- predicting real individual outcomes

Adaptive quitting should be interpreted with attention to autonomy, context, health, disability access, financial security, caregiving responsibility, discrimination, institutional responsibility, safety, support, opportunity, and the risk of shaming people for leaving harmful or misaligned conditions.
